How we work

From inquiry to delivered cartons

A four-step path from your first message to production and shipping.

01

Requirement and drawing

Send a drawing, a physical sample or a reference photo. Our engineers confirm dimensions, material and colour options before anything is quoted.

02

Pattern work and sample

We build the pattern, produce the sample and measure it against your specification so you approve a physical piece, not a drawing.

03

Mass production

Production runs in sequence: Raw Material Test → Cutting Fabric → Sewing → Size And Fits Inspection → Packing.

04

Packing and export

Finished goods are packed to your labelling requirement and dispatched through our freight partners to your market.
