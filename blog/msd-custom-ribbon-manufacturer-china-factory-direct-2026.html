Why choose a factory-direct ribbon manufacturer in China

When you order custom ribbon from a factory instead of a trading company, the entire production chain — yarn texturing, weaving, dyeing, printing, finishing and packing — stays in one place. That means your Pantone colour is checked at the source, your lead time is not extended by a sub-supplier handover, and your cost is transparent.

MSD Ribbon runs a 6,000 m² production floor in Tong'an Industrial Park, Xiamen, with 120 skilled workers and in-house weaving, dyeing, printing and bow-assembly workshops. Daily output is approximately 100,000 metres of ribbon, and the company exports to more than 80 countries.

What "custom" means at MSD Ribbon

A custom order can change any combination of: ribbon material, width, edge, finish, colour, print artwork, packaging and label. The most common programmes we run for global brand buyers are:

  • Custom-printed polyester, satin and grosgrain — your logo or pattern, Pantone-matched, on widths from 6 mm to 100 mm.
  • Custom-dyed solid colour — 196 stock colours on satin and grosgrain, plus custom Pantone from 2,000 yards per colour.
  • Wired-edge organza and satin — for floral, gift and holiday decoration.
  • Pre-tied bows and pull bows — self-adhesive, 1 to 6 inch sizes, in solid colour or printed.
  • Velvet ribbon — 49 stock colours on 1.5-inch width, 50-yard rolls.
  • RPET (recycled polyester) ribbon — GRS-certified, drop-in for ESG / SBTi commitments.

MOQ, lead time and sample policy

Sample: 3-5 business days for digital print, 7-10 days for woven or specialty finishes. Sample cost is refundable against the bulk order confirmation.

Bulk MOQ: 1,000 yards for custom-printed or custom-woven programmes; 500 yards for stock colour orders.

Lead time: 12-18 days for most custom orders, depending on material, width and quantity. Holiday-peak orders (Q3-Q4) should be placed 30 days in advance to lock production slots.

Payment: T/T 30% deposit + 70% balance before shipment, or L/C at sight for first-tier brand buyers.
